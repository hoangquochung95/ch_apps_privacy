# Privacy Policy – Phong Thuy AI

**Last updated:** May 02, 2026

**Phong Thuy AI** is developed by **Hung Hoang**. We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal information in accordance with applicable laws. This policy describes how we collect, use, and safeguard your data when you use the app.

---

## 1. Information We Collect

We may collect the following types of information:

- **Account information:** your email address and full name provided when you register or update your profile.
- **Login credentials:** hashed passwords managed securely through the Supabase authentication service.
- **Usage data:** information about the features you use within the app (feng shui analysis, hexagram lookup, compass, etc.).
- **Photos:** photos you take or select from your library for feng shui analysis. Photos are sent to our server for processing and are **not stored permanently**.
- **Transaction information:** in-app purchase (IAP) data via Google Play used to verify your subscription.
- **Device information:** device type, operating system, and app version used for debugging purposes.

---

## 2. Device Permissions

The app may request the following permissions on your device:

- **Camera:** to capture photos of spaces for feng shui analysis.
- **Photo library:** to select existing photos from your device.
- **Magnetometer (magnetic sensor):** to display the Ba Gua compass. Sensor data is **never transmitted** outside your device.

Permissions are used solely for the purposes stated above and are not shared with third parties.

---

## 3. How We Use Your Information

The information we collect is used to:

- Provide and maintain the feng shui analysis service.
- Authenticate your identity and manage your account.
- Verify in-app subscription purchases.
- Improve app quality and fix bugs.
- Provide support when needed.

---

## 4. Sharing Information with Third Parties

We **do not sell** your personal information. We only share data in the following cases:

- **Supabase** – cloud authentication and database platform. Data is encrypted and protected to SOC 2 standards. Privacy policy: https://supabase.com/privacy
- **Google Play (IAP)** – processes payments and verifies in-app purchase transactions. Privacy policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy
- **AI analysis server:** photos and parameters you enter (house facing direction, birth year, etc.) are sent to our server to generate analysis results. Data is used only for that specific request and is not stored long-term.

---

## 5. Data Storage and Security

- Account data is stored on Supabase with encryption at rest and in transit (TLS).
- Passwords are hashed and are **never** stored in plain text.
- We apply reasonable technical measures to protect data from unauthorized access.
- Data is retained for as long as your account is active or as necessary to provide the service.

---

## 6. Your Rights

You have the right to:

- **Access:** view the personal information we hold about you.
- **Edit:** update your full name in the Profile section of the app.
- **Delete your account:** request deletion of all your personal data by contacting us via email below.
- **Revoke permissions:** withdraw camera or photo library access at any time in your device Settings.

---

## 7. Children

This app is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect information from children. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us so we can address it promptly.

---

## 8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be communicated via email or an in-app notification. The "Last updated" date at the top of this page will be revised accordingly. Continued use of the app after changes are made constitutes your acceptance of the updated policy.
